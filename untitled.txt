1. INTRODUCTION & OBJECTIVE

The objective of this project phase is to ingest, clean, merge, and perform 
exploratory data analysis (EDA) on a benchmark news dataset containing both 
verified real news articles and known fake news articles. This serves as the 
foundation for training a supervised classification model to automatically 
detect fake news.


2. METHODOLOGY & DATA PROCESSING

* Data Acquisition: Loaded two distinct corpora comprising true news articles 
  (True.csv) and fake news articles (Fake.csv).
* Feature Engineering & Labeling: Created a binary numerical target column 
  named "category" where:
    - Fake news is encoded as 1.
    - Real news is encoded as 0.
* Dataset Integration: Merged both subsets vertically using Pandas DataFrame 
  concatenation, resulting in a unified dataset consisting of 44,898 total rows 
  and features including title, text, subject, date, and category.


3. EXPLORATORY DATA ANALYSIS (EDA) & VISUALIZATIONS

  to visualize the balance between real (0) and fake (1) news categories, ensuring 
  no extreme class imbalances that could adversely affect model training.
